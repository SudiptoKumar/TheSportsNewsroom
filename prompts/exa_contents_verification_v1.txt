THE SPORTS NEWSROOM V1: EXA CONTENTS VERIFICATION

PURPOSE
Use Exa Contents only on source URLs already discovered or otherwise explicitly identified. The goal is to extract evidence for one selected evergreen Sports & Games topic.

RULES
1. Verify the exact subject and central claim. Do not broaden into unrelated facts.
2. Prefer exact dates, names, measurements, rules, historical qualifiers, origin evidence, and official terminology.
3. Preserve important qualifiers such as earliest documented, first officially recognized, one of the oldest, or introduced under modern rules. Never silently upgrade a qualified claim into an absolute claim.
4. Prefer relevant highlights when they contain enough evidence. Use text when broader context is necessary.
5. Treat a source as evidence only for what it actually supports.
6. Do not infer unsupported numbers or dates.
7. If the source conflicts with another source, preserve the conflict for later review instead of choosing a winner without evidence.
8. Page images are optional enrichment. A missing image must never invalidate a verified story.
