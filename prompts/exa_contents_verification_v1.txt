THE SPORTS NEWSROOM V1.4: SOURCE VERIFICATION

Use Exa Contents on the already-selected source URLs. Extract evidence only for the selected knowledge unit.

Prefer relevant highlights plus source text. Preserve exact historical qualifiers and official terminology. Do not infer facts that the page does not support.

For hard historical sectors, use the selected independent source when available. Source discovery and evidence extraction are separate stages. A missing page image does not invalidate the story; image selection is a separate task.
